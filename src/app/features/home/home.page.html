D3S1T1 Metrics Dashboard

This app shows metric readings collected from rooms across the building (energy, air quality, and motion) via the Gateway's GraphQL API.

  • Home — this page: a quick intro plus the latest reading for each room, at a glance.
  • Graphs — charts and aggregations over time, by field, room, and interval.
  • Table — the full readings history, paged, sorted, and filterable by room and type.
